Can, must, should, might: every modal verb in pictures

160 patterns, one clear picture each, and three example sentences from easy to hard. 8 decks: ability, requests and permission, obligation and necessity, no obligation and prohibition, advice and suggestions, possibility and deduction, past modals, offers, habits and preferences.

  • Flip through cards and switch between easy, medium and hard sentences.
  • Type each sentence from memory with word-by-word feedback. A card counts as learned once all three sentences are typed, and your review pile is one tap away.
  • Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.
