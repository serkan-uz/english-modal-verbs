Privacy Policy

English Modal Verbs · Effective 10 October 2026

English Modal Verbs ("the app") is a flashcard app for learning English. This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Information the app collects

The app collects no personal information. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits that collect data.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Which example sentences you have typed correctly for each card, and the time of your last attempt.
  • Your preferred sentence difficulty level (easy, medium, or hard).

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time with the "Reset progress" action inside the app, or by deleting the app.

Network access

The app works fully offline. All flashcards, images, and example sentences are bundled inside the app. The app makes no network requests.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. It is suitable for all ages.

Third-party services

The app uses no third-party services. If you installed the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play, those stores may process data about your download according to their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue on GitHub.
